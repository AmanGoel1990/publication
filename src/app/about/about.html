About Us

Morarji Desai National Institute of Yoga (MDNIY) is an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Ayush, Government of India.

The institute works to advance yoga through education, training, research and public outreach, connecting the traditional knowledge of yoga with contemporary learning and practice.
